01 In plain terms

Most of my work starts with hardware and keeps going.

I work mostly on embedded systems and robotics: firmware, communications, sensors, control, test automation, and the software around physical systems.

I like being able to trace a problem from the hardware, through the firmware, and into the tools used to test it.

I also enjoy game development and visual work. UniQuest let me work on gameplay systems, pixel art, sound, and mobile controls in the same project.
