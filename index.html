05 / About

I like projects that make me move between layers.

I’m a Computer Engineering undergraduate at the National University of Singapore, also pursuing a second major in the Innovation and Design Programme.

Most of my work sits around embedded systems and robotics. I enjoy following a problem from a circuit or sensor, through firmware and communication, into the tooling that tests the finished system.

Outside that lane, I have built a Java CLI application, a cross-platform game, websites, and visual work for student organisations. The medium changes; I still care about making the result clear and usable.
